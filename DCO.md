# Developer Certificate of Origin policy

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0 -->

Morphoia Engine uses the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 (DCO), published at
<https://developercertificate.org/>. By adding a `Signed-off-by` trailer, a
contributor certifies that they have the right to submit the contribution under
the project's applicable license and that the contribution may be publicly
distributed.

Sign each commit with the contributor's real name and a reachable email:

```text
Signed-off-by: Full Name <email@example.org>
```

Git can add the trailer with `git commit --signoff`. The sign-off is a
provenance certification, not a copyright assignment. A maintainer must not
fabricate or add another person's sign-off. Contributions without a valid
sign-off require correction before integration; published history must not be
rewritten merely to repair an already integrated historical contribution.

Third-party material additionally requires source, exact license, version,
copyright notices, and compatibility review. DCO sign-off does not cure an
incompatible license or missing permission.
