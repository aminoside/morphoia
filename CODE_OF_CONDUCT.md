# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0 -->

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Morphoia Engine free of harassment for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples include demonstrating empathy and respect, giving and accepting
constructive technical feedback, focusing on evidence rather than status,
acknowledging mistakes, respecting privacy and data governance, and prioritizing
the community and scientific integrity over individual advantage.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized conduct,
trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing private information,
misrepresenting evidence or authorship, pressuring others to expose restricted
data, and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner or, when available, the
repository's private reporting channel. Do not include sensitive medical or
personal data in a public report. The project owner may remove content, restrict
participation, or refer serious matters to the relevant platform or authority.
Enforcement will be proportionate, documented, and mindful of reporter privacy.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1,
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
